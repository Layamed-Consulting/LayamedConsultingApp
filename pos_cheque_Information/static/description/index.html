Cheque Payment

Go to Point of Sale → Black List and enter the details such as Identifiant, Client name, Comment, and Status (Ok or KO).

This screen shows the information that you entered into the Blacklist.

In the POS session, if the same Identifiant is entered, a dangerous warning message is displayed.

If we cancel the form of cheque, the payment line is automatically removed.

In the POS session, if the Identifiant is not blacklisted, no dangerous message is shown.

After placing the order, go to POS Backoffice → Orders → Select your transaction → Go to Payments and click to view full cheque details.

Bank Card Payment

When you select payment as Bank Card, you are prompted to enter the STAN number.

A success message confirms the STAN was entered successfully.

After placing the order, go to POS Backoffice → Orders → Select your transaction → Go to Payments and click to view full bank card details.

And here you can see the same STAN that the cashier entered.

Bank Transfer - Virement

When selecting Bank Transfer as the payment method, enter the required details:
- Numéro de virement
- Nom du client
- Montant
- Réf de la commande
- Date de la commande

If we cancel the bank transfer form, the payment line is automatically removed.

After placing the order, go to POS Backoffice → Orders → Select your transaction → Go to Payments and click to view full bank transfer details.
